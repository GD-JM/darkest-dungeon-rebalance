[h1]Darkest Refined[/h1]
[i]Familiar heroes, new possibilities. A rebalance that keeps the original tension and adds depth to every choice.[/i]

Darkest Refined reworks and rebalances the skills of all 20 heroes in Darkest Dungeon.

It starts from the fun of building a party. In the original game, Vestal, Jester and Shieldbreaker were close to the one right answer and handled almost any situation. Each hero's distinct character is one of the game's strengths, but as parties settled into that lineup, the character was buried.

So other heroes' healing was raised so they can stand in for the Vestal, and other heroes' stress healing so the Jester is no longer required. The Shieldbreaker was brought down in line with her official lore and other heroes' growth curves, and other heroes were raised by that share. Marking parties, once a niche, are no longer tied to the Arbalest, Houndmaster, Occultist and Bounty Hunter: many more heroes have stronger marking abilities.

[h2]How it is tuned[/h2]
This is not a patch that shakes up balance on gut feeling or annoyance. Each change was checked for whether it is justified and whether it fits the hero's official lore, so the original's depth stays intact.
[list]
[*][b]Only what can be justified.[/b] Only changes explained by comparison with other heroes, official lore and the original game's number rules made it in.
[*][b]Works the way it looks.[/b] Art and names deliver what they promise. Battlefield Bandage cures bleed and Battlefield Medicine cures blight, splitting the work. The warlike Hellion no longer becomes exhausted, and deals less damage in return.
[*][b]Not locked into four skills.[/b] Unused skills were raised and skills that had become the default were lowered, so different situations call for different skills. The Vestal, once locked into Judgement, Dazzling Light and her two heals, now has Judgement's self-heal moved to Mace Bash, Dazzling Light limited to ranks 1 and 2, and Divine Grace usable from any rank.
[/list]

[h2]What you can try[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Healing is shared across heroes.[/b] Arbalest's Battlefield Bandage now cures bleed, and Plague Doctor's Battlefield Medicine rises from 1–3 to 3–7 healing and takes on blight. Both heal 3–7, making them healers just short of the Vestal. In the original, the only real stress healers were the Jester and the Crusader. Now Houndmaster's Cry Havoc (4–6), Arbalest's Rallying Flare (2–4, herself included), Man-at-Arms' Bolster (1–3, no per-battle limit), Flagellant's Endure (net 8 from level 1) and Suffer (4–6), and Crusader's Inspiring Cry (5–9) can all be the stress healer a party needs.
[*][b]Many heroes can build a marking party.[/b] Musketeer's Mark the Quarry is now a mark that cuts PROT by 20–30% instead of dodge, and Bounty Hunter's Mark for Death raises damage taken by 15–25% instead of cutting PROT, so the whole party benefits from the mark. Antiquarian's Nervous Stab, which had no mark, now marks at level 5.
[*][b]Pull the back line in and finish it.[/b] Skills that pull enemies grew from four in the original to eight. Arbalest's Bola, which knocked back ranks 1 and 2, now pulls an enemy in ranks 2–4 forward 2, and Plague Doctor's Disorienting Blast, which shuffled at random, now stuns and pulls 2. Duelist's Disengage and Shieldbreaker's Expose also pull an enemy forward 2.
[/list]

[h2]Two reworks in focus[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Antiquarian goes all the way to the Darkest Dungeon.[/b] She stays weak up to Veteran; at skill level 5 her skills gain new effects, and from Champion she pulls her weight with her class trinkets. At level 5, Protect Me gives whichever ally guards her 3 turns of riposte.
[*][b]The Musketeer hides her allies.[/b] All seven skills she shared with the Arbalest were rebuilt. Covering Smoke is the only skill that stealths an ally, for 2 turns, and Piercing Ball pierces 25–50% PROT on any rank from 1 to 4.
[/list]

[h2]Still refining. Still listening.[/h2]
The goal is perfect balance. I will keep refining the patch to come closer to that ideal, and gather your feedback to find the answers together. Tell me about skills that feel too strong or too weak, skills with no reason to equip, and combinations that work well. Hero and skill levels, trinkets, party and battle situation help my judgement a great deal.

[h2]Installation and compatibility[/h2]
[b]All five DLC heroes must be enabled:[/b] Flagellant (The Crimson Court), Shieldbreaker, Duelist, Runaway, Musketeer (free).

[b]Do not enable it together with Every Hero Worth Taking or the per-hero standalone editions (links at the bottom).[/b] They change the same hero files.

Can be applied to an existing estate. With UI Enhancement, Musketeer skills may display their original names.
