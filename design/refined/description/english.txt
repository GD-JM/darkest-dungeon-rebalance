[h1]Darkest Refined[/h1]
[i]Familiar heroes. New possibilities. A carefully tuned hero rebalance.[/i]

The heroes you always bring. The four skills you always pick.
What if every other choice had a real reason too?

Darkest Refined carefully tunes the heroes and skills of Darkest Dungeon so you can plan and test parties of your own. Healing and stress relief no longer rest on one or two heroes, support skills open the way for physical attackers, and each hero's strengths are tuned to work with their allies.

The original difficulty and tension come first. Every buff carries a price, and weakened roles find new uses. Game data, class trinkets, official lore and real play are reviewed together, and every change keeps a written reason. Your experience and reasoned feedback keep refining it.

[h2]How it is tuned[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The same tension.[/b] Buffs and nerfs are designed together, so the game stays as hard as you remember.
[*][b]Clear guardrails.[/b] Class trinkets and each hero's official lore set the limits of every change, so nothing feels foreign.
[*][b]Evidence, not instinct.[/b] A comparison model of damage, healing, armor reduction and actions guides the numbers; real play and your feedback correct them.
[/list]

[h2]What you can try[/h2]
Ranges cover skill levels 1–5.
[list]
[*][b]Healing, shared.[/b] Healing used to rest on the Vestal, the Occultist and the Jester; now many heroes share the load, making the game more flexible and parties more varied. Crusader's Battle Heal restores 3–4 HP (6–7 at level 5) and raises the target's PROT. Arbalest's Battlefield Bandage cures bleed, and Plague Doctor's Battlefield Medicine cures an ally's blight. Antiquarian's Fortifying Vapours amplifies all healing the target receives, stacking. Arbalest's Rallying Flare relieves 3–5 stress for the party, and Man-at-Arms' Bolster has been reworked into a stress-healing skill.
[*][b]Break the armor.[/b] As armor rises with difficulty, physical attackers other than the Shieldbreaker and the Grave Robber were often left behind. Now support heroes can strip enemy armor for them: Occultist's Weakening Curse (PROT −25 to −35%), Shieldbreaker's Puncture (−30 to −40%), Leper's Sunder [b](NEW SKILL)[/b] (−30 to −50%) and Vestal's Illumination (−15 to −25%) open armored enemies for physical attackers.
[*][b]Another way to play.[/b] Highwayman's Tracking Shot now tracks the enemy's movements to ready a riposte, and Duelist's Advance grants him a duelist's combat buffs. Shieldbreaker's Pierce can no longer reach every enemy rank; instead the dancer of her story slips freely between friend and foe, leaving her footprint with every step. Musketeer is fully reworked: from the back she pierces armor and hides allies in smoke.
[/list]

[h2]Two reworks in focus[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Antiquarian earns her place at Champion.[/b] Up to Veteran she stays weak, in exchange for the extra gold she brings. From Champion, her class trinkets bring her close to other heroes. Rather than growing strong herself, she makes her teammates stronger, so she is worth taking into the Darkest Dungeon.
[*][b]Abomination: restraint becomes power.[/b] His human form trades a little accuracy on Manacles for a harder-hitting Beast's Bile. The stress he keeps in check with Absolution pays for the transformation and turns into the beast's healing, damage, speed and CRIT, easing the burden on allies.
[/list]

[h2]Installation and compatibility[/h2]
[b]Enable all five DLC heroes:[/b] Flagellant (The Crimson Court), Shieldbreaker, Duelist, Runaway and Musketeer (free). The Musketeer rework is included; no external mod is required.

Can be enabled in an existing estate. Other reworks replacing the same hero, effect or buff files may conflict. With UI Enhancement, Musketeer skills may display their original names.
