# Rebalance (Verified) — Patch notes

- **Shieldbreaker · Pierce** [SB-002] (G-04, R-VALUE-20, P-09): Target ranks 1-2-3-4 → 1-2-3, crit modifier +5–9 → +2/3/4/4/4. Melee + armor piercing + reaching every rank was the core Shieldbreaker problem; a melee skill reaching rank 4 is a rare premium, so rank 4 is removed.
- **Shieldbreaker · Weapon speed** [SB-011] (G-04, R-GROW-03): Weapon speed 5/6/8/8/9 → 5/5/6/6/7. Brings an exception back to the base game's growth rule (level-1 value + floor((n−1)/2)).
- **Flagellant · Bleed resist** [FLA-001] (G-04, R-RES-01): Bleed resist 65 → 60. Matches the base game's cap for a hero's signature resist (baseline +30).
- **Flagellant · Weapon speed** [FLA-007] (G-04, R-GROW-03): Weapon speed 6/6/8/8/9 → 6/6/7/7/8. Brings an exception back to the base game's growth rule.
- **Houndmaster · Blight/Bleed resist** [HM-017] (P-22, R-RES-01): Blight/Bleed resist 40 → 30. In the base game a resist only deviates from baseline when the hero's lore says so; this +10 has no such line (Trap +30 stays, backed by the guard-dog text).
- **Houndmaster · Cry Havoc** [HM-014] (G-02, G-05, P-12): Levels 1–4: stress heal 2/3/4/5 → 4/4/5/5, chance 66/68/70/72% → 70/71/72/73% (level 5 stays 74%, so every level still grows). Fixes only the low-level unreliability of the party-wide stress heal.
- **Houndmaster · Blackjack** [HM-013] (R-STUN-01, R-STUN-03, P-09): Accuracy 95 → 90, Strong stun → normal stun (damage −65% unchanged). It was the only melee stun that both reaches rank 3 and stuns at the strong tier; now on the baseline for damaging stuns (accuracy 90, normal stun).
- **Man-at-Arms · Weapon damage** [MAA-001] (G-01, P-03): Weapon max damage 9/10/12/13/14 → 10/12/13/15/16 (min unchanged). He sat furthest below the HP-to-damage trend across all 20 heroes; 5-10 is the existing preset of comparable heroes (Bounty Hunter, Highwayman).
- **Duelist · The Boot** [DUE-006] (R-STUN-01, R-STRAT-01): Damage −40% → −60%. A melee skill that stuns and pushes at once, yet it gave up less damage than other stuns. −60% sits inside the damage range of stun skills (−40 to −75%).
- **Hellion · Barbaric YAWP!** [HEL-001] (P-19, G-02, R-STRAT-01): Stun, exhaustion and the 3-per-battle limit removed → self buff (accuracy +5, damage +20%, once per use) + all enemies deal 10–20% less damage (3 turns) + corpse clear; targets ranks 1-2 → all ranks. The un-cleansable, battle-long exhaustion (−20% damage, −3 speed) contradicted the relentless art.
- **Hellion · Breakthrough** [HEL-002] (P-19, G-02): Exhaustion removed, damage −50% → −55%. It was the weakest damaging AoE hitting ranks 2 and 3 (exhaustion cost most of its value).
- **Hellion · Bleed Out** [HEL-003] (P-19, P-21): Exhaustion removed, damage +20% → 0%, crit modifier +6–10 → +10–14. Its rank-1-only target is now paid back with crit instead of damage; crits extend the bleed, which suits late-game armored enemies.
- **Hellion · Adrenaline Rush** [HEL-004] (G-05, P-12): Its self buff moved to YAWP, leaving cure + self heal → self heal 1/2/2/3/4 → 3/4/5/6/7.
- **Hellion · Wicked Hack** [HEL-005] (G-02): Accuracy 85 → 90, making her basic attack reliable now that exhaustion is gone.
- **Arbalest · Bola** [ARB-004] (P-01): Level 5 push now uses the intended effect (1D 105% → 1E 115%). Bug fix: 1E exists in the base game but was never linked.
- **Abomination · Beast's Bile** [ABO-002] (P-23, G-01): Accuracy 95 → 90, damage −90% → −75%. Human-form accuracy comes down and base output goes up, closing the gap with beast skills (accuracy 85–90).
- **Abomination · Manacles** [ABO-005] (P-23, R-STUN-03): Accuracy 95 → 90, the baseline for damaging stuns. Damage unchanged; his large weapon already puts it near the top of stun skills.
- **Abomination · Slam** [ABO-001] (P-23, G-03): Usable ranks 1-2-3 → 2-3-4, debuffs removed, accuracy 80 → 90, damage −25% → 0%. The beast had nothing to do from rank 4; now it slams from the back and moves forward.
- **Abomination · Transform** [ABO-004] (P-23, P-12): Self stress relief 4–8 on transforming; beast form stress per turn 6 → 4. The cost to allies stays — this gives a reason to transform at all.
- **Grave Robber · Poison Dart / Thrown Dagger** [GR-001] (R-SKILL-05, P-11): Poison Dart blight 4 turns → 3 (the standard DoT duration); in exchange the self accuracy buff moves from Thrown Dagger to Poison Dart.
