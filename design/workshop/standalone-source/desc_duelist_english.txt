[h1]Darkest Refined No.05 · Duelist[/h1]
A master of the blade who moves between two stances. In Defensive Stance she evades, draws attacks with a mark and answers them with Riposte. In Aggressive Stance she kicks enemies into a stun and finishes them with Coup de Grâce. She is one of the strongest heroes in the original game, and that strength sat more in her attacks outside the riposte and in her dodge than in the riposte itself.
[list]
[*][b]Riposte chances stay[/b] The combat-start Riposte (2 rounds) and Anticipation's Riposte are untouched. Feint only lowers riposte damage at low levels.
[*][b]Attacks outside the riposte and dodge are nerfed[/b] The Boot, Flèche and Coup de Grâce lose damage, and Touché loses dodge.
[*][b]Effects that move the enemy line[/b] Disengage pulls one enemy in ranks 2–4 forward 2 ranks as she retreats, and The Boot pushes 2 ranks in exchange for its lower damage.
[/list]
Overall this is a nerf.
