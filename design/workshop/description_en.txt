[h1]Every Hero Worth Taking[/h1]
[i]Keep each hero's identity. Give more heroes, skills and parties a reason to be chosen.[/i]

A rebalance of the 15 base-game heroes, built around party variety. The goal is to make your favorite heroes work together without relying on a particular healer or the same four skills. Broader healing and stress relief, armor-breaking support for physical attackers, and more useful rank choices open new ways to play.

[h2]More choices, familiar tension[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Buffs and nerfs are designed together.[/b] Damage, accuracy, reach and support have trade-offs. Redistributing power across the roster aims to preserve the base game's overall difficulty and tension.
[*][b]Hero identity and class trinkets matter.[/b] Changes respect familiar skills and lore, while checking that existing class trinkets retain meaningful uses.
[*][b]Numbers inform playtesting.[/b] A comparison model evaluates damage, healing, PROT reduction and action opportunities. Its calculations guide decisions; real play and feedback keep improving them.
[/list]

[h2]What the published build changes[/h2]
Ranges below cover skill levels 1–5. ACC and CRIT are level-1 skill values unless stated otherwise.
[list]
[*][b]More ways to sustain a party.[/b] Crusader's Battle Heal restores 3–4→6–7 HP and raises the target's PROT, supporting a primary-healer build with healing trinkets. Antiquarian's Fortifying Vapours heals and amplifies all healing the target receives, stacking with each use. Arbalest's bandage cures bleed; Rallying Flare guarantees stress heal 2/2/3/3/4 for the whole party, including herself. Man-at-Arms brings repeatable Bolster.
[*][b]Support opens armored targets to physical attackers.[/b] Occultist's Weakening Curse reduces PROT by 25–35 points; Illumination combines dodge reduction, destealth and Burn 1/1/2/2/3. Retribution reduces one target's PROT by 15–25 points; Bellow instead lowers enemy ACC by 5–10 for 2 turns. Nervous Stab has no armor reduction.
[*][b]Different builds for familiar heroes.[/b] Highwayman's Tracking Shot prepares riposte without moving. Wicked Slice has ACC 90, DMG 0%, CRIT 5% and rank-3 reach; Open Vein keeps vanilla DMG −15%, CRIT 0%, higher ACC and bleed support. Bounty Hunter's Flashbang works from ranks 2–4 and Caltrops from any rank. Jester's Inspiring Tune works from ranks 3–4; Solo works from any rank and shuffles enemies instead of slowing them. Point Blank Shot pushes 3 ranks. Leper's Chop and Hew have ACC 80, Purge and Sunder ACC 90; Sunder: PROT −30–50 points for 3 turns; debuff chance 130–170%.
[*][b]Vestal chooses between support and setup.[/b] Illumination has ACC 95, reduces dodge and applies Burn; Hand of Light has ACC 90 and DMG −40%, dealing damage while preparing her next attacks.
[/list]

[h2]Antiquarian and Abomination: the direction[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Antiquarian grows through her teammates.[/b] Skill levels 1–4 stay weak in exchange for extra gold. Level 5 brings a deliberate leap: a 2-turn mark, self-mark removal and ally riposte unlock; healing received rises from +20% to +40%, and Blight lasts 5 turns. Flashpowder lowers MOVE resist by 20/22/25/27/30 points for 2 turns, with Burn 1/1/2/2/7. Class trinkets support this party-focused growth, giving her a reason to enter the Darkest Dungeon.
[*][b]Abomination makes transforming a real choice.[/b] His human form has lower accuracy but stronger base performance. The stress he manages with Absolution pays for the transformation and turns into the beast's healing, damage, speed and CRIT, easing the burden on allies.
[/list]
